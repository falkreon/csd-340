Top 5 Favorite Foods

I don't know if these are my absolute favorites, and I'm not sure if these are in order. These are just what I could come up with on short notice that I thoroughly enjoy.

  1. Spinach Calzone (or any calzone really. Probably swap the frozen dough in the recipe for canned pizza dough to make prep easy)
  2. Makizumi or nori roll sushi, such as spicy tuna, or spider maki (don't make these at home - ingredient availability is poor here and the linked recipe was the *least horrifying* one I could find)
  3. Basically any kind of Pasta Al Pesto (careful with this recipe's size, it's huge!)
  4. Crab rangoon
  5. Falafel (boxed falafel mix works just fine too, and pan fried is delicious!)
